# 貧人約瑟（[香港，約1851年？]，牛津大學博德利圖書館 Sinica 1191） / Pin ren yue se [Poor Joseph]: Transcription Dataset

《貧人約瑟》是書目推定約1851年在香港刊行的漢文宗教短篇，描述倫敦傭工約瑟偶入禮拜堂、病中和教師對話、臨終前分出積蓄濟貧。底本為牛津大學博德利圖書館 Sinica 1191，封面展覽標籤164號與1876年費城百年展書目相符。展覽書目將此冊歸於俾士（George Piercy），可供研究宗教小冊、漢文翻譯與十九世紀香港印刷史。

Pin ren yue se (Poor Joseph) is a Literary Chinese tract catalogued as published in Hong Kong around 1851. It follows a London labourer who hears a sermon by chance and, in his final illness, arranges to give part of his savings to people in need. The Bodleian Libraries copy, Sinica 1191, bears a Philadelphia Centennial Exhibition label numbered 164 that matches the catalogue’s attribution to George Piercy. The text supports research on missionary tracts, Chinese translation, and nineteenth-century Hong Kong printing.

## V0.2.0

6 幅館方影像中含封面及三葉六面正文，附封籤、版心、印記與後加題識。提供逐欄對應原圖的 TXT、JSON 和 TEI XML。比對獨立原圖讀法後回圖裁定，採用 7 筆訂正，留存疑讀定位。

The dataset covers all six source images, including the cover and the six printed faces on three leaves. TXT, JSON and TEI XML link the transcription, title slip, running titles and copy-specific marks to the images. An independent image reading was compared with the first transcription. Source-image adjudication adopted 7 changes and retained unresolved readings.

## 資料與原件 / Data and source

TXT 供逐欄閱讀，JSON 保留來源欄位，TEI XML 提供文字層與影像對應。疑字在文字層與定位層保存。影像由館方 IIIF 取用。

The TXT, JSON and TEI XML share scan and column references. Source images are available through the library’s IIIF service.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。保留原欄序及可辨原字。原刻頓記、封題、版心、展覽標籤、鉛筆註與藏印可分層定位。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Retains column sequence and legible source forms. Printed punctuation, cover title, running titles, exhibition label, pencil notes and the seal are separately locatable. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
